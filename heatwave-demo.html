    Decision translation model

    Official signals stay official; local context makes them actionable.

    01

    Anchor forecasts in observed water state

    UFZ soil-moisture percentiles, DWD crop-specific nFK, RADOLAN 24-hour rain, and DWD station temperatures provide a dated baseline for 614 sub-basins.

    02

    Carry uncertainty through local views

    ICON-D2-EPS covers the near term; ICON-EU/Global EPS extends the horizon. Daily means and hourly member standard deviations are area-weighted into 400 NUTS-3 regions and 16 states.

    03

    Apply crop and soil context safely

    Farmers can select DWD-supported crops, growth stage, and a soil scenario. The result changes prioritisation and checks, never an automatic irrigation volume or harvest decision.

    Germany already has mature DWD heat and rain warnings, DWD and UFZ soil-water services, state flood portals, and specialised agricultural advice. HeatLens's role is to connect these services to transparent, role-specific decision checks. Its 0-100 values are uncalibrated screening indices, not probabilities, official warnings, flood forecasts, or agronomic instructions. Observations validate and initialise context but do not turn the index into a calibrated forecast. HydroBASINS citation: Lehner & Grill (2013).
