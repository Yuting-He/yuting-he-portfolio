    Decision translation model

    Official signals stay official; local context makes them actionable.

    01

    Screen both sides of water stress

    DWD ICON heat, atmospheric demand, root-zone moisture, daily water balance, and hourly rain intensity feed separate dry and excess-water indices for 614 sub-basins.

    02

    Aggregate to 400 local regions

    Sub-basin predictions are weighted by exact EPSG:3035 intersection area into NUTS-3 districts and urban districts, then rolled up to the 16 states.

    03

    Translate signals into staged checks

    Residents, farmers, and municipal teams receive different reversible actions, verification steps, and explicit gates for costly or official decisions.

    Open-data architecture

    A production-shaped separation of geometry, forcing, model, and action.

    Germany already has mature DWD heat and rain warnings, DWD and UFZ soil-water services, state flood portals, and specialised agricultural advice. HeatLens's role is to connect these services to transparent, role-specific decision checks. Its 0-100 values are uncalibrated screening indices, not probabilities, observations, official warnings, flood forecasts, or agronomic instructions. HydroBASINS citation: Lehner & Grill (2013).
