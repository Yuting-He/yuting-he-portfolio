# Technical roadmap

1. **Explicit repair workflow.** Add uncertain fault verification, a limited repair crew, dispatch, travel, deterministic then empirically fitted repair durations, repair completion and service recalculation. Track customer-minutes interrupted by integrating actual connectivity loss over time. Do not infer restoration from inspection.
2. **Utility topology and electrical validation.** Import sanitized feeder GIS and asset registries with provenance; validate normally open switches, source availability and critical loads. Compare connectivity screening with distribution load flow, voltage and thermal constraints. Utility engineering review remains essential.
3. **Calibrated hazards and observations.** Fit severity priors and logistic coefficients to historical events with train/event separation. Add out-of-sample reliability diagrams, Brier/log scores, spatial correlation diagnostics, sensor confusion matrices and sensitivity analysis. Explicitly represent missing and out-of-distribution data.
4. **Operational mission constraints.** Add weather-based no-fly gating, airspace/obstacle routing, communication outages, uncertain travel time, battery telemetry and safe landing sites. Charging requires demonstrated energized infrastructure and approved hardware; it cannot be assumed after outages.
5. **Decision science.** Test alternative repair-support utilities and multiple-step planning against the same paired scenarios. Report policy regret against small exact or exhaustive cases, wall-clock cost, robustness to misspecified priors and bootstrap paired effects over many independent events.
6. **Human-operated pilot.** Read-only integration with outage management and distribution management systems; audit logs, access control, scenario versioning and operator overrides. Begin in shadow mode and compare recommendations against operator decisions before enabling any dispatch capability.
7. **Deployment and resilience.** Package a signed offline desktop bundle, pinned dependency wheelhouse and reproducible environment. Add exportable evidence trails, fault-tolerant persistence and reviewed data retention. Keep the simulation engine modular before considering services or distributed infrastructure.

The present prototype has no autonomous dispatch authority and is not a substitute for utility procedures or flight authorization.
