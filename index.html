HeatLens Germany - Multi-Hazard Decision Translation

Built a multi-scale Germany-wide GIS research app with 614 hydrological sub-basin prediction units, 400 NUTS-3 district and urban-district response regions and 16 state summaries, joined through exact area intersections. Live DWD/RADOLAN observations, UFZ soil percentiles, crop-specific DWD soil water and ICON ensemble-member dispersion screen heat, dry stress and excess water while official DWD and state services remain authoritative. A transparent action policy translates the evidence into different low-regret checks for residents, farmers and municipal teams, with crop/root-zone scenarios and human gates for consequential decisions.
